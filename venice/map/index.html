Traghetto — the €2.50 crossing
What it isShared gondola crossing the Grand Canal at 7 fixed points. €2.50 (€2 for residents). Stand in the boat. No booking, no schedule.
Best crossingSanta Sofia – Pescaria (near Rialto market). Most reliable.
