What Changed
Verified August 2026
Venice Access Fee — The Biggest Change
Day visitors · Historic centre only · Status as of August 2026
The Access Fee trial has ended. The Venice Access Fee (Contributo di Accesso) ran on 60 specific dates between 3 April and 26 July 2026, in a two-tier system: €5 if booked at least 4 days in advance, €10 if booked within 3 days. As of 27 July 2026 the trial period is over — the City of Venice states on its official site that it is currently possible to visit Venice with no payment or registration required, for day visitors and overnight guests alike.

What this means for your trip: if you are visiting any time from August 2026 onward, do not pay any third-party site claiming to charge an "access fee" or "entry ticket" for Venice — none is currently due. Be alert to scam sites that kept the old booking pages live and may still try to charge you.

Looking ahead to 2027: whether the fee returns is a decision for Venice's city council; as of August 2026 nothing has been confirmed or scheduled. Check cda.ve.it a few weeks before any 2027 trip for the latest status — we will update this page as soon as a decision is announced.
€5 early / €10 last-minute (Apr–Jul 2026) → Not charged since 27 Jul 2026 cda.ve.it
Vaporetto & Transport
ACTV — single ride
Single ride confirmed at €9.50. This remains one of the most expensive single public-transport tickets in Europe — a 10-minute trip costs the same as a 40-minute one. Any visit involving more than two rides in a day makes a pass better value. Buy passes at ACTV booths (Piazzale Roma, Ferrovia, Rialto, San Marco) or via the AVM Venezia Official App. Always validate before boarding — inspectors check regularly; fines are €60 plus the fare.
€9.50 single — confirmed actv.avmspa.it
ACTV — tourist time passes
All pass prices reconfirmed unchanged for 2026. The clock starts at first validation, not at purchase. Buy in advance but validate only when you are ready to travel. Passes cover vaporetti, mainland buses (Mestre), and the People Mover. Not valid on Alilaguna (airport service), lines 16 and 19, or water taxis.

Museums & Attractions
Palazzo Ducale (Doge's Palace)
Price increased to €35 (standard) — or €30 if booked online at least 30 days before your visit. The ticket still covers Museo Correr (integrated route), Museo Archeologico, and the Biblioteca Marciana. Valid 3 months, one entry per site. In high season, book online early for both the discount and timed entry that reduces indoor queuing. The Itinerari Segreti (Secret Itineraries guided tour) supplement was not independently re-verified this update — the book's €28 figure has been left unchanged; confirm on the Musei Civici website before booking.
€30 → €35 (€30 online, 30+ days ahead) visitmuve.it
Basilica di San Marco — entry is no longer free
The biggest single change at this sight: since 1 January 2026, on-site ticket sales have ended and entry requires a paid, timed-entry ticket booked in advance. Basilica-only admission is €10. Combined tickets: Basilica + Pala d'Oro €20; Basilica + Museum and Loggia dei Cavalli €20; full ticket (Basilica, Pala d'Oro, Museum and Loggia) €30. The Bell Tower (Campanile) is a separate €15. Book only at the official site, tickets.basilicasanmarco.it — the book's €3 skip-queue reference is out of date. Shoulders and knees must still be covered — enforced at the door. Bags larger than a small purse must be checked at the free deposit nearby (Ateneo San Basso, Piazzetta dei Leoncini). No photography inside.
Free entry (book states) → €10 basilica-only, up to €30 combined tickets.basilicasanmarco.it
Alilaguna water bus — airport to Venice
Price increased to €18 one-way, €32 return. Boats every 30 minutes from approximately 06:00 to midnight. The Orange Line reaches San Marco in 75 minutes; the Blue Line via Lido in 90 minutes. Note: Alilaguna is not covered by ACTV passes. The ATVO bus to Piazzale Roma is confirmed at €12 one-way on the official fare table — still the cheaper option, taking about 25 minutes to Piazzale Roma. ATVO return-ticket pricing was not independently re-verified this update; check atvo.it before buying.

New for 2026
Added August 2026
Access Fee — the 2026 trial has ended
The Venice Access Fee applied on 60 dates between 3 April and 26 July 2026. Since 27 July 2026 it is no longer being charged, and the City of Venice has not announced whether or how it will return in 2027. If you are travelling before this page's next scheduled review (January 2027), assume no fee applies — but re-check cda.ve.it close to your travel dates, since the city council could reinstate it with limited notice.
Basilica di San Marco — now a mandatory paid ticket
As of January 2026, walking up and entering the Basilica for free is no longer possible. Every visitor needs a timed-entry ticket (from €10) booked in advance at tickets.basilicasanmarco.it. On-site ticket sales have ended. Build this booking into your itinerary before you leave home, especially for peak-season dates.
San Marco Basilica — bag deposit before entry
Bags larger than a small purse or daypack are not permitted inside and must be checked before entry at the Ateneo San Basso on Piazzetta dei Leoncini, about 1 minute from the main entrance. The service is free. Factor this into your timing: the deposit has its own queue in peak season. Backpacks, rolling luggage, and large shoulder bags are all affected.
AVM Venezia Official App — buy passes on your phone
The official ACTV app now allows purchasing and validating vaporetto passes directly on your phone without needing a physical card or queuing at booths. Download AVM Venezia Official before arrival. The app also shows real-time vaporetto arrivals at every stop — useful for avoiding long waits when a different line or walking would be faster.
Seasonal Alerts
Updated August 2026
Dates that will affect your visit
Venice is affected by its own events, Italian national holidays, and (if reinstated) the Access Fee calendar. These are the dates that require specific planning, updated here for the 2027 travel window this page covers.
!
Carnevale (usually late January – mid-February; 2027 culminates on Martedì Grasso, 9 February 2027) Crowds rival August, prices match. Piazza San Marco becomes impassable during peak Carnevale afternoons. If you want Carnevale, book accommodation 3–4 months ahead; prices triple. If you do not want Carnevale, avoid the two weekends and the week before Ash Wednesday. This window is a reliable estimate calculated from the 2027 Easter date; the city confirms the exact programme closer to the season.
!
Easter weekend (27–29 March 2027) Italian families flood the city for the long weekend. Restaurants require reservations days ahead. Piazza San Marco impassable. Book accommodation months ahead. Whether an Access Fee applies to these dates depends on whether Venice reinstates the fee for 2027 — unconfirmed as of August 2026; check cda.ve.it before booking.
!
April 25 + May 1 (fixed Italian public holidays) Liberation Day and Labour Day fall on the same calendar dates every year. In 2027, April 25 is a Sunday and May 1 a Saturday, so the "ponte" bridge-weekend effect Italians build around these dates is smaller than in years when they fall midweek — but both remain high-demand travel dates nationwide, and accommodation prices rise accordingly.
!
Festa del Redentore (third weekend of July — expected around 16–18 July 2027, exact date confirmed by the city closer to the date) Saturday night fireworks over the Giudecca Canal are extraordinary. Boats crowd every waterway. The city fills with Venetians reclaiming their city for one night. If you want the fireworks, arrive before 22:00 and stay on the Zattere or Giudecca waterfront.
!
Acqua alta season (October–March) With MOSE now operational, the most extreme events (>130 cm) are largely prevented. Moderate events (100–110 cm) still flood Piazza San Marco and low quaysides for 2–3 hours. Download the Ci.Venezia app. Rubber overshoes (€5–10) available everywhere in the city. Follow the raised passerelle walkways.
!
New Year's Eve — Piazza San Marco concert A public concert and midnight fireworks. The piazza fills from early evening. Prices spike from Christmas through January 6 (Epiphany) and then drop sharply. January 2–early February is among the cheapest and quietest periods of the year.
✓
Best timing for most visitors — late May, early June Spring warmth, long days. Crowds below summer peak. The city is fully operational. Outdoor dining works. Islands are rewarding.
✓
Best low-cost window — November (excluding Salute weekend) to mid-December Minimum tourists, lowest prices, atmospheric fog. Acqua alta possible but manageable with MOSE. The Accademia without a queue. Bacari without a wait. The city feels inhabited again.
