The Yamanote Line — Learn This First
What it isA circular JR loop connecting every major area. Shinjuku, Shibuya, Harajuku, Tokyo Station, Ueno, Akihabara — all on the same loop.
FrequencyEvery 2–4 minutes. No timetable needed. Just go to the platform.
Key timesShinjuku ↔ Shibuya: 7 min. Tokyo Station ↔ Ueno: 7 min. Shibuya ↔ Asakusa: 25 min (via Akihabara).
NightLast trains around 00:30–01:00. After that: taxi or walk. Never assume the next train is coming.

IC Cards (Suica / Pasmo)
EssentialBuy at any station on arrival. Load ¥3,000–5,000. Works on every train, metro, bus, and most convenience stores.
2026 noteSome foreign Visa/Mastercard can now load Suica via Apple/Google Wallet — check your bank app before arriving.
TaxiStart meter ¥500–730. Honest and safe. Use for last trains or luggage days. Never share with strangers.
Airport Transfers
Narita (NRT)Narita Express (N'EX) to Shinjuku/Shibuya: 80–90 min, ¥3,250. Skyliner to Ueno: 36 min, ¥2,570. Budget 2 hours to central Tokyo.
Haneda (HND)Keikyu Line to Shinagawa: 13 min, ¥310. Monorail to Hamamatsucho: 18 min, ¥500. Budget 45–60 min to centre.
