Prices & hours changed

Verified Aug 2026
Rome

Colosseum + Roman Forum + Palatine Hill

Standard adult entry. Online advance booking is mandatory — no walk-up tickets at the gate. Timed slots fill 2–3 weeks ahead in summer. The official seller is no longer CoopCulture — book only at ticketing.colosseo.it (plus a €2 non-refundable booking fee); sites ending in anything other than colosseo.it are not official.

€18 → €24 ticketing.colosseo.it

Vatican Museums + Sistine Chapel

Walk-in queue effectively gone in high season — same-day tickets often sold out by 9 AM. Standard admission is €20 on site; online booking with skip-the-line access is €25. Book only at tickets.museivaticani.va — lookalike domains charge well above the real price.

€17 → €20–25 museivaticani.va

Borghese Gallery

2-hour timed entry strictly enforced. No exceptions. Book the 9:00 AM slot as the guide recommends. Standard ticket is €16 plus a mandatory €2 online reservation fee.

€15 → €18 galleriaborghese.cultura.gov.it

Pantheon

Free for years, then €5 from 2023. The standard ticket rose again on 1 July 2026. Reduced €2 for EU citizens 18–25, free under 18. Book at museiitaliani.it — recommended, still not mandatory for individual visitors.

€5 → €7 museiitaliani.it
Florence

Uffizi Gallery

Closed Tuesdays (unchanged). Walk-up queue averages 70–90 minutes July–August. Pre-book. On-site same-day ticket is €25; online advance purchase is €29.

€20 → €25–29 uffizi.it

Accademia Gallery (David)

Closed Mondays. Book at least 48 hours ahead — runs out faster than the Uffizi. Same-day on-site ticket is €16 if available; online booking (recommended) is €20 including the reservation fee.

€12 → €16–20 galleriaaccademiafirenze.it
Venice

Day tripper access fee (Contributo di accesso)

Not a blanket Fri–Sun charge — in 2026 it applied only on 60 scheduled days between 3 April and 26 July, tiered: €5/day if booked 4+ days ahead, €10/day if booked closer to the date. The 2026 trial ended on 27 July — as of this update, no fee is being charged. Overnight guests, children under 14, and the lagoon islands (Murano, Burano, Lido, etc.) are exempt whenever the fee is active. Whether it returns for 2027 has not been decided yet — check cda.ve.it before you travel.

€5–10 (2026 trial) → Not currently charged cda.veneziaunica.it
Naples & Campania

Pompeii Archaeological Park

Summer hours (16 March–14 October): last entry 5:30 PM, closing 7:00 PM, as the guide states. The base "Pompeii Express" ticket (ancient city only) is €20; add the suburban villas (Villa of the Mysteries) for €25 total, or top up on site with an €8 supplement. Tickets now sell exclusively through Vivaticket via pompeiisites.org.

€16 → €20 pompeiisites.org

National Archaeological Museum of Naples (MANN)

Closed Tuesdays. Open Wed–Mon 9:00 AM–7:30 PM, last admission 6:30 PM. Secret Cabinet included in standard admission except on free-entry days, when it closes.

€15 → €20 coopculture.it

Active scams & warnings

Verified Aug 2026
Rome

Termini area — Plainclothes police impersonation

Men show unofficial badges and ask to inspect your wallet for "counterfeit euros." This is not a real police procedure. Walk toward the station entrance. If pressured: "chiamo la polizia."

Colosseum exterior — Fake skip-the-line tickets

Street vendors sell "official" tickets for €35–60 in cash. No authorised resellers exist outside the official site. Book only at ticketing.colosseo.it or licensed tour operators.

Spanish Steps / Trevi Fountain — Friendship bracelets

Men place woven bracelets on your wrist, then demand €10–20. Keep hands in pockets near these sites. Decline firmly before any contact is made.

Florence

Piazza del Duomo — Clipboard distraction

Groups ask you to sign a petition while an accomplice pickpockets you. Do not engage. Active near the Duomo and Ponte Vecchio since 2023, still common in 2026.

Venice

Gondolas — Official 2026 rate

Fixed municipal rate: €90 daytime (8:00 AM–7:00 PM) / €110 evening (7:00 PM–8:00 AM), max 5 passengers (capacity was cut from 6 for safety), 30 minutes. Extension: €40 per extra 20 minutes. Any higher quote is above the legal tariff. Non-negotiable.

Naples

Centrale Station — Unsolicited car "minders"

Men offer to "watch" your parked car for cash. Organised extortion. Use only car parks with automatic barriers and a staffed cashier.

Sicily

Circumetnea Railway — Catania segment

Since March 2026, the Catania–Santa Maria di Licodia section is closed for Catania metro extension works and replaced by a bus link. From Catania, take the metro to Nesima, then the replacement bus to Santa Maria di Licodia (about 40 minutes, via Paternò, Valcorrente, Piano Tavola, Misterbianco) to rejoin the train for the rest of the circuit. The €13 day pass still applies. No confirmed end date for the works — check circumetnea.it before you go.

All destinations

Restaurant cover charge — "pane e coperto"

€1.50–4 per person, added automatically. Legal and standard — not a scam. What is a scam: restaurants that won't show a menu with prices before you sit. The law requires visible pricing. Walk past.

New since publication

Added Aug 2026
Rome

Trastevere — Gelateria update

Fior di Luna (Via della Lungaretta 96) has changed ownership. Quality declining. Best alternative: Fatamorgana, Prati branch (Via Trionfale) — unchanged, still the best natural-ingredient gelato in Rome.

Testaccio market — Worth adding to your map

The Testaccio covered market (Via Galvani) is now significantly more food-forward. Stalls 15 and 23: best supplì and trippa in Rome. Mon–Sat 7:00 AM–3:00 PM. Cash only.

Florence

Uffizi — Afternoon discount

Since 1 January 2026, entering the Uffizi from 4:00 PM onward costs a flat €16 — well under the standard €25–29. Best if you only want 2–3 hours and don't mind a later start; check the current afternoon slot times on uffizi.it before booking.

Naples

Quartieri Spagnoli — New find

Trattoria da Emilia (Via Speranzella 33) — opened 2025. No tourist menu. Lunch €12–15, cover included. Real Neapolitan kitchen. Closed Sundays.

Venice

Cannaregio — Neighbourhood alert

Cannaregio now the most reliable area for non-tourist-trap dining in Venice. Guide's Dorsoduro picks remain valid — add a dinner walk through Rio della Sensa if staying 4+ days.
